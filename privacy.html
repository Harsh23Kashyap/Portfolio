What this site does not do

Your theme preference

The light and dark mode switch saves your choice in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify. To deliver pages, Netlify processes standard connection data such as IP address and browser type in its server logs. Netlify handles this under its own privacy policy.
